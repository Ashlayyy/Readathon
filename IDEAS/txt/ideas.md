# After the Crucible — January event ideas

Working title: **Daggerheart** (Daggermouth / the Heart — book-club callback)

**Timeline**
- Readathon / Crucible ends: **31 December**
- Sequel event: **January** (likely needs more than 2 months if the full card + story version ships — treat January as Act 1 or plan longer)

---

## Single story (condensed)

The Crucible ends. Top reader(s) from each realm become **the Heart** (Royals) — ~6 people. Everyone else becomes **Rebels**.

The Heart cannot attack like normal sabotage; they heal / fortify themselves and can use **toxicity** to steal Rebel health (framed as cutting food, water, soldiers, executions). Rebels must attack the Heart and work together.

Progress unlocks **milestones**: notebook scraps (fantasy detective plot) + **cards** (powers). Rebel cards = survival (shields, rations, shelter). Heart cards = empire (search, cut water, executions). Choice cards fork the path with hidden prices. Hosts can still play if one is Heart and one is Rebel and each writes fog-of-war for the other side.

December crowns the Royals. January decides if the Heart keeps the world — or the Rebels burn the throne.

- [x] **Cards** with art + ability + story scrap (Pokémon-card energy)
- [x] **Rare cards** + **intentional duplicates** (e.g. same card can drop ~3 times) for frustration/hype
- [x] **Notebook / milestone clues** both sides collect and assemble together
- [x] Faction must **work as a team** (share clues, shield each other)
- [x] Need a dedicated **plan night** (worldbuilding + what’s locked vs maybe vs no)
